# Aryan AI And Analytics Note

## AI-Backed Research Features

Aryan's product work connects the watchlist and company research views to the existing intelligence pipeline:

- ESG momentum scoring combines environmental, social, governance, AI-adoption, and controversy measures.
- The company Copilot uses stored signals, evidence, and the most recent score to answer basic investor questions with grounded context.
- Scheduled news ingestion classifies RSS headlines into persisted company signals, which appear on company and News & Alerts views.
- Quantitative company analytics provide ESG trend slope, momentum acceleration, downside risk, risk-adjusted momentum, signal quality, and evidence coverage.

## Watchlist Interpretation

The Watchlist page does not invent investment advice. It presents current quote movement alongside persisted ESG and risk metrics so an investor can compare tracked companies and open the detailed analysis page when a movement needs investigation.

## Delivery Behavior

Built-in notifications, including watchlist additions, alert events, market-open notices, and admin broadcasts, are mirrored to the in-app inbox, email, and the linked Telegram chat. User notification preferences still allow email or all notifications to be disabled.
